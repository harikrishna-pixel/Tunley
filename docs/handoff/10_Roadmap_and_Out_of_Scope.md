# 10 · Roadmap and Out of Scope

## Not in V1.0

- Banner and app-open ads (V1.0 uses only the ad break and rewarded ads)
- Cloud sync, social login (referral + optional email account added in V1.3)
- Downloading from URLs / YouTube / TikTok links (App Review and copyright risk)
- Apple Music / streaming songs (DRM, impossible)
- Ringtone catalogue / library of ready-made tones
- AI voice covers, vocal remover, noise reduction
- Voice recorder
- Speed / pitch change
- iPad layout, landscape
- Android (architecture is ready; Kotlin engine later)

## V1.1 (after launch data)

- **Share extension** "Convert with Tunely" from Photos / Files / other apps
- Speed and pitch (Pro)
- AIFF output and custom bitrate / sample rate (Pro), with their own UI
- Recorder (make a ringtone from your voice)
- Localisation: Spanish, Portuguese (BR), German, French, Japanese, Korean
- Paywall A/B tests via RevenueCat Experiments (based on `08` funnels)

## V1.2+

- Vocal remover / noise reduction (on-device ML)
- Android release (Media3 Transformer + LAME on Android)
- Widgets (Recent files, "Make a ringtone")
- Shortcuts / App Intents ("Convert latest video to MP3")
